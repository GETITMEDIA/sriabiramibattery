Who we are

Exclusive EXIDE Battery Dealer in Mayiladuthurai

Established in 2005, M/s Sri Abirami Battery is an Authorised Dealer of Exide Industries Limited in Mayiladuthurai, Tamil Nadu. We operate an exclusive showroom located opposite Amma Unavagam, General Hospital Road, Mayiladuthurai – 609001, offering prompt supply and service of all types of Exide Batteries and Inverters.

01. Our Offerings

We provide a wide range of Exide battery solutions for homes, commercial setups, industries, and solar applications. Our offerings include automotive, inverter, solar, submarine, and industrial batteries.
